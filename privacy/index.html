RaptorDash — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

RaptorDash does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

The app displays telemetry sent by RaptorDash Bridge, which runs on your own PC. That data travels directly between your PC and your phone over your own local network. It never reaches us and is never stored anywhere else.

Your settings and your purchase state are kept on your own device. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see payment details.
