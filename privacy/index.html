Privacy Policy

RaptorDash and RaptorDash Bridge · Last updated 7 October 2026

RaptorDash does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

Where your telemetry goes

The app displays telemetry sent by RaptorDash Bridge, which runs on your own PC. That data travels directly between your PC and your phone over your own local network. It never reaches us and is never stored anywhere else. We operate no server that the app or the bridge connects to.

What stays on your device

Your settings — language, units, chosen layout — and your purchase state are kept on your own device. Purchases, when they become available, are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

What the bridge does on your PC

RaptorDash Bridge reads telemetry from the racing games installed on your PC, and may change those games' own telemetry settings so that they start sending it. It does not read anything else on your computer, and it sends nothing outside your local network.

Permissions

The phone app uses the local network to reach the bridge, and keeps the screen awake while a dashboard is open. It asks for nothing else.

Children

RaptorDash is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes — if an analytics tool or advertising were added, for instance — this page will be updated before that version is released.
