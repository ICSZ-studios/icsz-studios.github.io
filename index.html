Your phone, as a sim racing dashboard.

RaptorDash shows live telemetry from the racing game on your PC — speed, revs, gear, lap and stage times, tyre and brake temperatures, pressures, damage, fuel and g-force — on the phone sitting beside your wheel.

Download the bridge for Windows All releases

Free. Windows 10 or later. Installs per-user, no admin rights needed.

How it works

  • 01

    Install the free RaptorDash Bridge on the PC you play on.

  • 02

    Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi network. The PC is found automatically.

  • 03

    Start your game. The bridge switches that game's telemetry on for you.

  • 04

    The dashboard fills in, and changes shape to suit the game.

Privacy

Telemetry never leaves your network. It travels directly from your PC to your phone over your own Wi-Fi. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising, and nothing is sent to a server of ours — because there isn't one. Read the privacy policy.
