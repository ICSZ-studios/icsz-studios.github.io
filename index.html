Your phone, as a sim racing dashboard.

RaptorDash shows live telemetry from the racing game on your PC — speed, revs, gear, lap and stage times, tyre and brake temperatures, pressures, damage, fuel and g-force — on the phone sitting beside your wheel. In Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator it is also a button box, a gamepad and a head tracker.

Download the bridge for Windows All releases

Free. Windows 10 or later. Installs per-user, no admin rights needed.

How it works

  • 01

    Install the free RaptorDash Bridge on the PC you play on.

  • 02

    Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi network. The PC is found automatically.

  • 03

    Start your game. The bridge switches that game's telemetry on for you.

  • 04

    The dashboard fills in, and changes shape to suit the game.

For the trucks

  • BUTTON BOX

    Lights, blinkers, hazards, wipers, retarder, cruise control, engine, lift axles, cameras, seat and mirrors, one touch each. Every key lights up from the truck's real state. Build your own page with only the keys you use.

  • DRIVE WITH YOUR PHONE

    Tilt it to steer, with two thumb pedals for throttle and brake. No driver to install and no changes to your control settings.

  • HEAD TRACKING

    Prop the tablet under your monitor and its front camera turns your view with your head, through opentrack. The status bar tells you whether the game is following.

The bridge installs its own input plugin for the two truck games from its Button box window. Restart the game once afterwards. Head tracking needs opentrack, which the bridge can download from opentrack's official release and set up for you.

Several devices at once

One Google account, every device. Pro belongs to your Google account, so install RaptorDash on each phone and tablet signed in with it. They all connect to the same bridge at the same time, each doing its own job: one as the dashboard, one as the button box, one as the gamepad and a tablet as the head tracker.

The button box, gamepad and head tracking are for Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator; the dashboard works with every supported game.

Privacy

Telemetry never leaves your network. It travels directly from your PC to your phone over your own Wi-Fi. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising, and nothing is sent to a server of ours — because there isn't one. Head tracking uses Google's face detection on the tablet itself: the camera picture never leaves the device, though Google's library sends Google usage diagnostics that do not identify you.
